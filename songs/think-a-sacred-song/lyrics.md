# Think a Sacred Song

Hymns—for Home and Church, no. 1006. English, both verses. Verified October 24, 2026 against [official Gospel Library lyrics](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/music/hymns-for-home-and-church/think-a-sacred-song?lang=eng) and the original program presentation. ## Verse 1 — block A

Think a sacred song;
the words will give you pow’r.
Think the melody, and in that very hour

## Refrain — block B

Your heart and mind will open
to let the Spirit in,
And you will feel His comfort again.

## Verse 2 — block C

Think a sacred song
when scared or feeling bad.
Hum the quiet tune
whenever you are sad.

## Refrain — block D

Your heart and mind will open
to let the Spirit in,
And you will feel His comfort again.

Performance: A → B → C → D. No additional refrain, introduction lyrics, bridge or coda is specified in the source. The final three lines of each verse share the same wording and musical function; this documentation calls them the refrain for slide grouping.
