# I Love to See the Temple

Children’s Songbook, no. 95. Words and music: Janice Kapp Perry. Verified 2026-10-24 against official Church source. Both verses complete; retain original presentation punctuation and display breaks. Two four-line blocks per verse, no repeated chorus.

## Verse 1, block 1

I love to see the temple.
I’m going there someday
To feel the Holy Spirit,
To listen and to pray.

## Verse 1, block 2

For the temple is a house of God,
A place of love and beauty.
I’ll prepare myself while I am young;
This is my sacred duty.

## Verse 2, block 1

I love to see the temple.
I’ll go inside someday.
I’ll cov’nant with my Father;
I’ll promise to obey.

## Verse 2, block 2

For the temple is a holy place
Where we are sealed together.
As a child of God, I’ve learned this truth:
A fam’ly is forever.
