# I Will Shine

Words and music by Shawna Belt Edwards.

## Source and selected version

Verified 2026-10-24 against the original program slides and the [songwriter's original-recording lyrics](https://shawnaedwardsmusic.com/pages/i-will-shine). This program uses the original recording version, including “the lonely soul” and the separate “In every time, / In every place,” phrases. The [Friend arrangement](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/friend/2022/01/i-will-shine?lang=eng) instead uses “a lonely soul” and “In every time and every place”. Preserve the existing program's chosen version and punctuation. No hymn number assigned here.

## Performance structure

Verse 1 → chorus → verse 2 → chorus → closing repetition. Closing repeated line occurs only at the end, as in the existing program.

### Verse 1

There’s a light within me,
The perfect light of Christ.
And I have made a promise
To keep it burning bright

By walking in His footsteps
And following His plan

In every time,
In every place,
In every way I can.

### Chorus

I will shine like a candle in the dark.
All it takes
to make a difference is a spark.

And it’s easy to see
that the brightest I’ll be
Is when I let His light shine through me.

### Verse 2

One simple act of kindness
Will lift the lonely soul.
One gentle word that’s spoken
Turns sadness to hope.

One choice to be like Jesus will
Spread the love He gives
And one by one the world becomes
A better place to live.

### Final chorus with closing repetition

I will shine like a candle in the dark.
All it takes
to make a difference is a spark.

And it’s easy to see
that the brightest I’ll be
Is when I let His light shine through me.
When I let His light shine through me.
