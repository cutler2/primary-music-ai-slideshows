# Holding Hands Around the World — storyboard

**Status: complete (September 2026).** Kept as a worked example of the method. **This cast and setting belong to this song only; do not reuse them for another song** unless a connected series is explicitly requested.

**Hymn 1011**, *Hymns—For Home and Church* · Words and music: Janice Kapp Perry, 2001 · © 2001 Janice Kapp Perry; may be copied for incidental, noncommercial Church or home use. Written for the satellite broadcast marking Primary's 125th anniversary. Related scriptures: Alma 53:20–21; 1 Timothy 4:12; Psalm 127:3–5; Matthew 18:3–5, 10.

**Wording verified:** the older *Friend* sheet music opens both verses "There are children"; hymn 1011 reads "We are children." The deck uses the hymnbook wording.

Full lyrics are not stored in this public repository. Slides are identified by opening words and key word.

**Scope:** both verses, chorus generated once and reused. **21 unique images + title image; 26 lyric positions.** Every slide scored 4 or higher; 12 scored 5.

## Decisions and lessons

- **No figure in any light.** Slides 11 and 13 were checked for human shapes in light or sky; regenerate rather than crop.
- **Ordinance restraint.** The covenant slide shows the greeting after a baptism: no font, no water, no raised hands.
- **A vivid setting took over the cue.** An earthquake setting for WORK made the disaster, not the work, the memory. Changed to flood cleanup with no standing water or collapsed buildings.
- **A symbol pointed at the wrong doctrine.** "A gift to give" was first a seedling in cupped hands, which cues *faith* and duplicated other hands-offering slides. Re-keyed to COVENANT.
- **Temple accuracy.** The St. George Utah Temple has no angel Moroni, and generators keep adding one. The deck used a non-identifiable spire instead.
- **Cast sheet prompt.** Carry viewing conditions, not song theme; theme made the generator decorate the sheet with flags and globes.
- **Aspect-ratio drift.** Output silently switched from 16:9 to 3:2 mid-session; nine images were cropped, losing 11% each. Check dimensions on every image.
- **Style drift across days.** Later images came back more rendered and photo-like; appending the style tail fixed it.
- **Consistency.** Tools that accept a reference image hold the cast better; in ChatGPT, stay in one thread and reference the cast sheet each time.

## Assembly

- 13.333 × 7.5 in slides; each image composited at 2000 × 1125 with a burned-in bottom scrim (bottom 40%, to alpha 240, easing exponent 1.15).
- Lyrics Arial Bold 40 pt white, centered, bottom-anchored box at x 0.55, y 5.20, 12.233 × 1.95 in; manual breaks at phrase boundaries.
- Title slide: bright art, so the band lifts to cream (#FDFAEE, alpha 150) with dark type (#27321B); title 48 pt, credit 16 pt.
- Grid review deck: 2 × 2 full-bleed quadrants (each quadrant of 16:9 is 16:9); lighter scrim 32% / alpha 225; captions 15 pt.
- PNG export: LibreOffice headless → PDF → `pdftoppm -png -r 144` → 1920 × 1080.

Deliverables (in the music leader's local folder): main deck (27 slides), grid deck (9), 36 PNGs, MP4 lyric video.
